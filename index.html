Nuclear Reaction Energetics

Pick a target, a projectile and an ejectile, set the energy of the beam in the laboratory frame, and the whole energy balance of X(a,b)Y* is redrawn to scale from the ENSDF mass excesses and adopted levels.

 

        Adopted levels from ENSDF. The ground state leaves W1 = 0 and Q1 = Q0.

        What the diagram draws

        ECM = mama + mX Ea    Edisp = mXma + mX Ea

        E*(C) = Sa + Edisp    Q0 = Δ(mX) + Δ(ma) − Δ(mY) − Δ(mb) = Q1 + W1

        Q1 = (1 + mbmY) Eb − (1 − mamY) Ea − 2√(mamb)mY √Ea√Eb cos θ

        The app solves that relation for Eb; both roots are reported when the ejectile is confined to a forward cone. Switching on relativistic kinematics replaces it with the exact invariant-mass solution, which is also what is used whenever a photon is involved.

        Data. Mass excesses, spins, parities, half-lives and adopted levels come from the IAEA Live Chart of Nuclides API, which serves ENSDF together with the AME2020 mass evaluation distributed with it.

        Conventions. Atomic mass excesses are used throughout, so the electron masses cancel in Q0; electron binding is neglected. Energies are kinetic unless marked as excitation.
